
# Equipment and Customer Management

## Equipment Management

The Equipment Management module allows administrators to maintain equipment inventory.

Features:
- Add equipment records
- View equipment information
- Update equipment details
- Delete equipment records when permitted
- Search equipment
- Monitor total and available quantities

## Category Management

The Categories Management module organizes equipment into categories.

Features:
- Add categories
- View category records
- Update category information
- Delete categories when permitted
- Search categories

## Customer Management

The Customers Management module maintains customer information.

Features:
- Register customers
- View customer records
- Update customer information
- Delete customer records when permitted
- Search customers

## Database Integration

These modules use JDBC to communicate with the MySQL database.

Related database tables:
- equipment
- categories
- customers
